# Design System

## Overview

Jay Me Test is a polished mobile H5 quiz app for Jay Chou fans. The visual identity is light, ceremonial, and compact: a centered mobile shell, warm off-white canvas, white cards, thin borders, and a gold music-note badge. The product message is built around three modes: Classic Mode, Album Challenge, and Endless Abyss. The page feels like a fan exam with collectible progress rather than a generic quiz form.

## Colors

- **Primary Accent**: `#CA8A04` - gold title, active navigation, primary buttons, score highlights.
- **Accent Light**: `#E2B93B` - logo gradient highlight and button sheen.
- **Accent Dark**: `#9A6B03` - gradient depth for gold surfaces.
- **Primary Background**: `#F8FAFC` - clean mobile page canvas.
- **Deep Background**: `#F1F5F9` - subtle cool page depth.
- **Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF` - quiz mode cards, login prompts, stats panels.
- **Primary Text**: `#020617` - strong headings and mode names.
- **Secondary Text**: `#475569` - supporting product copy.
- **Muted Text**: `#94A3B8` - labels and quiet helper text.
- **Info Blue**: `#1E3A8A` - Classic Mode icon.
- **Abyss Purple**: `#7C3AED` - Endless Abyss icon and border highlight.
- **Abyss Red**: `#DC2626` - danger accent for Endless Abyss.

## Typography

- **Heading Serif**: Noto Serif TC / PingFang SC fallback. Used for the gold title and promotional headline moments; high weight, refined, ceremonial.
- **Body Sans**: Noto Sans TC / PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei fallback. Used for labels, cards, CTAs, and app UI copy.
- **Display Numbers**: Noto Serif TC. Used for stat values such as `4`, `4`, and `30`; use tabular numbers in video callouts.
- **Hierarchy**: video hero text should be 88-132px; card labels 26-40px; small UI labels no smaller than 20px.

## Elevation

Depth is quiet and tactile. Cards use `#FFFFFF` surfaces, thin cool borders, soft shadows, and rounded corners around 14-16px. The app avoids heavy drop shadows; separation comes from subtle contrast, light background gradients, and gold/purple accent rings.

## Components

- **Gold Music Badge**: circular ring with a gold music note; floats above the title as the brand device.
- **Mobile H5 Shell**: narrow 390-480px app viewport centered on wide screens; use this as the recognizable product frame.
- **Mode Cards**: white rounded cards for Classic Mode, Album Challenge, and Endless Abyss; icons are compact, tinted blocks.
- **New Ribbons**: diagonal `NEW` ribbons on Album Challenge and Endless Abyss; signal expansion beyond the basic quiz.
- **Stats Tiles**: three compact numeric tiles for cumulative challenges, best streak, and average score.
- **Login Prompt Card**: centered trophy/avatar card with gold primary CTA and outlined secondary CTA.
- **Bottom Tabbar**: fixed mobile navigation with Home, Ranking, and Profile icons.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use a light off-white canvas and white card surfaces.
- Let `#CA8A04` gold carry brand moments, CTAs, and the final title.
- Show the actual captured mobile screens generously; the product is the interface.
- Pair refined serif headlines with practical sans UI labels.
- Use subtle arcs, rings, and music-note motifs to imply Jay Chou fandom without using copyrighted imagery.

### Don'ts

- Do not turn the promo into a dark neon music video; the captured app is light and refined.
- Do not use generic blue SaaS gradients or purple-blue hero gradients.
- Do not make text-only beats; every beat should include a screen, badge, card, or UI asset.
- Do not over-round cards beyond the app's 14-16px radius.
- Do not invent celebrity photos, album art, or licensed Jay Chou imagery.
